## 4. Cookies, pixels and tracking

4.1 We use cookies and similar technology (pixels, SDKs, local storage) in three groups:

| Group | What it does | Needs your consent? |
|---|---|---|
| Strictly necessary | Checkout, login, security, remembering your cookie choice | No |
| Analytics | Counts visits and shows how pages are used, via PostHog | Yes in the EU/UK; opt-out in the US |
| Advertising | Meta Pixel, TikTok Pixel, Google Ads and YouTube tags, and similar tools that measure ad results and build audiences | Yes in the EU/UK; opt-out in the US |

4.2 **EU and UK visitors:** non-essential cookies stay off until you accept them in our cookie banner. You can change your choice at any time through the "Cookie settings" link in our site footer.

4.3 **US visitors:** advertising cookies may be active by default. You can turn them off through "Cookie settings" or the "Do Not Sell or Share My Personal Information" link. We treat a **Global Privacy Control (GPC)** signal from your browser as a valid opt-out of sale and sharing for that browser.

4.4 Some browsers send "Do Not Track" signals. There is no common standard for them, so we rely on GPC and your cookie settings instead.

## 8. Your rights in the EU, EEA and UK

8.1 Under GDPR and UK GDPR you have the right to:

- **access** your data and get a copy;
- **correct** inaccurate or incomplete data;
- **delete** your data, where we no longer need it or have no legal basis to keep it;
- **restrict** our use of it while a complaint is checked;
- **port** data you gave us, in a machine-readable format;
- **object** to uses based on legitimate interests, and to direct marketing at any time;
- **withdraw consent** at any time, without affecting earlier processing.

8.2 **How to ask.** Email help@propagandistan.com with "Privacy request" in the subject. We may ask you to confirm your identity, usually from the email address on your order. We reply within one month. For complex or numerous requests we may extend this by up to two more months and will tell you why.

8.3 Some rights have limits. For example, we must keep tax records for the periods in section 7 even if you ask us to delete them.

8.4 **Complaints.** You can complain to the data protection authority where you live or work, or where you think a breach happened. In the UK, that is the Information Commissioner's Office (ico.org.uk). EU authorities are listed by the European Data Protection Board (edpb.europa.eu). We would appreciate the chance to resolve your concern first.

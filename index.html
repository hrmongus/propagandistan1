FAQ

Questions people ask before they switch.

Do you take a cut of membership revenue?

No. On Creator and Studio the platform fee is 0%. You pay the flat plan price and standard card processing fees, which go to the payment processor, not to us.

Can I import members from another platform?

Yes. Upload a CSV of emails and we send a one‑tap invitation. Paid members re‑enter card details once because processors don’t transfer stored cards between merchants.

What happens if I cancel?

Your page stays live on the Starter plan with your existing free members. Paid tiers pause. You can export everything, members included, at any time.

Do I need to know how to code?

No. Everything is configured from the dashboard. If you do want to build on top of it, Studio includes API access.

Where is my data hosted?

In the EU. We don’t sell, share or train on member data. Full details are in the privacy policy linked below.
